## 2. Full Description (Play Store Ready)

### English:
⚡ **Boost your typing speed 10x with WordBoard — The Universal Text Expander!**

Tired of typing the same messages, email addresses, meeting links, and customer support replies over and over? WordBoard lets you assign short trigger codes (like `/addr` or `omw`) and magically expands them into full sentences, formatted templates, or multi-line paragraphs!

🌟 **Works with EVERY Language in the World (Unicode UTF-8)**
Unlike other expanders that only support English, WordBoard is truly universal. Create shortcuts and expand phrases in:
- 🇮🇳 Hindi (हिन्दी / देवनागरी)
- 🇸🇦 Arabic & Urdu (العربية - full RTL support)
- 🇯🇵 Japanese (日本語 - Kanji, Hiragana, Katakana)
- 🇨🇳 Chinese (中文 - Simplified & Traditional)
- 🇪🇸 Spanish (Español)
- 🇧🇩 Bengali (বাংলা)
- 🇷🇺 Russian (Русский)
- 🇫🇷 French, 🇩🇪 German, Tamil, Telugu, and all world scripts!

🎯 **Smart Keyboard Pop-up Pill**
Keep your favorite keyboard (Gboard, Samsung Keyboard, SwiftKey, etc.)! As you type, a sleek Material 3 pop-up pill appears right above your keyboard showing the expansion preview. Tap once to expand, or choose instant auto-expand!

✨ **Intelligent Typo & Misspelling Fixer**
Typed a word wrong or made a typo in your shortcut? WordBoard detects the mistake and shows a quick 1-tap correction pill directly above your keyboard!

🕒 **Dynamic Variables**
Insert live real-time information into your snippets:
- `{date}`: Automatically inserts today's date in your locale format
- `{time}`: Automatically inserts current time
- `{clipboard}`: Pastes your latest clipboard item
- `{cursor}`: Places your cursor exactly where you want to start typing

🔒 **100% Privacy Focused & Safe**
Your privacy is our highest priority:
- All phrases and shortcuts are stored 100% locally on your phone.
- No keystrokes, personal passwords, or sensitive data are ever logged or transmitted over the internet.
- Zero tracking, zero ads, zero battery drain.

## 3. Google Play Accessibility Policy Compliance (MANDATORY)

To comply with Google Play's 2026 Accessibility Service Policy, include this exact declaration in your Play Console submission:

> **Accessibility API Usage Declaration:**
> "WordBoard uses the Android AccessibilityService API strictly to detect user-configured shortcut triggers (such as `/addr` or `omw`) in active text input fields across applications, and to provide the user with a 1-tap floating expansion pop-up or automatic text replacement.
> 
> WordBoard does NOT use the Accessibility API to:
> - Change user settings without permission
> - Record, collect, or transmit user keystrokes, passwords, or personal data
> - Circumvent Android privacy controls or show unauthorized advertisements
> 
> A clear and prominent in-app disclosure is presented to the user before requesting this permission."

## 4. Play Store Graphic Assets Guidelines
- **App Icon:** 512 x 512 px PNG (32-bit with alpha)
- **Feature Graphic:** 1024 x 500 px JPG or PNG
- **Screenshots:** Minimum 4 screenshots (1080 x 1920 px or 1080 x 2400 px):
  1. Home Screen with categorized multilingual phrases
  2. Floating pop-up pill active above Gboard / Samsung keyboard
  3. Universal language pack selector (Hindi, Arabic, Spanish, Japanese)
  4. Dynamic variable template editor (`{date}`, `{time}`, `{clipboard}`)
